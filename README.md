**The World in One Day** — a flat editorial poster that sums up the world news of Monday 23 November 2026 in a single 1024×1024 image. The hero panel covers the collapse of the COP31 climate summit in Antalya, Türkiye — the first such failure in 17 years, ending after record overtime with the Antalya Pact unadopted. The panels below cover the World Court ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya (9–6, the first state ever held responsible), US Democrats clinching 258 House seats for their largest majority since 1992, Nvidia's board voting unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO 11 days after he resigned, and a 7-year-old boy pulled alive from the rubble in Dymer after a Russian strike — with a ticker for BepiColombo reaching Mercury, the Nikkei's 5.06% fall, and Delta's outage-delayed flights. The poster is drawn programmatically (Python/PIL) so every label stays crisp, and `main.ts` copies it to `/output/entry.png`.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
